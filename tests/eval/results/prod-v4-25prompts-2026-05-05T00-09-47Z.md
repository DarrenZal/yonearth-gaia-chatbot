**Response (first 800 chars):**

> To make biochar at home, you can follow these steps:
> 
> 1. **Start with Biomass**: Gather organic material like wood chips, straw, or coconut husks.
> 
> 2. **Prepare a Container**: Use a metal drum or a steel can with a lid to contain the biomass.
> 
> 3. **Create a Fire**: Heat the biomass in the container without oxygen, a process called pyrolysis.
> 
> 4. **Monitor the Process**: Allow the biomass to heat up slowly and release gases, leaving behind biochar.
> 
> 5. **Cool and Store**: Once the process is complete, let the biochar cool before storing it in a dry place.
> 
> Remember, safety is key when working with fire. For more detailed guidance, you can refer to resources like "The Biochar Handbook" by Kelpie Wilson or reach out to biochar experts like those at High Plains Biochar (biocharco.op.com).
