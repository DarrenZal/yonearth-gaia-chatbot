Tell me about biodynamic farming.
🌱
Biodynamic farming is a holistic approach to agriculture that treats the farm as a self-contained, self-sustaining organism. It was developed by Rudolf Steiner in 1924 and builds on organic practices by incorporating cosmic rhythms, biodynamic preparations, and strong soil stewardship. Several Y on Earth guests, including those working with Demeter USA, have spoken about the certification process and why it goes beyond organic.

What about biochar?
🌱
Biochar is a stable form of carbon produced by heating organic material in a low-oxygen environment (pyrolysis). When added to soil it sequesters carbon for centuries while improving soil fertility, water retention, and microbial habitat.
